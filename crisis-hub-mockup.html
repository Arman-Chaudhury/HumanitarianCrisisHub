DEMAND CHANGE
01

Call your congressional representatives to demand increased humanitarian funding — the UN appeals are chronically underfunded.

02

Identify yourself as a constituent — offices prioritize calls from their district. One call takes two minutes.

03

Urge support for targeted sanctions against Myanmar's military junta and Sudan's RSF commanders responsible for atrocities.

04

Push for enforcement of the Uyghur Forced Labor Prevention Act (UFLPA) and demand corporate supply chain audits.

05

Attend town halls — ask your question on the record, in public. Organize call-in days with friends.

06

Follow up — one call is good. Repeated contact over weeks is what actually moves offices to act.

CALL SCRIPT

Adapt for any crisis. Calls are more effective than emails.

"Hi, my name is [Your Name] and I'm a constituent from [City, State]. I'm calling to urge [Representative/Senator Name] to [specific ask — e.g. support a ceasefire resolution, increase humanitarian funding, impose targeted sanctions]. The humanitarian situation in [Crisis Region] is dire, and I believe the United States has a responsibility to act. Thank you for your time."

INTERNATIONAL HUMANITARIAN LAW

International Humanitarian Law (IHL), also known as the laws of war, seeks to limit the effects of armed conflict. It protects civilians and restricts means of warfare. The Geneva Conventions of 1949 and their Additional Protocols are its core treaties.

Key principles: distinction (civilians vs combatants), proportionality (force vs military advantage), precaution (minimize civilian harm), and the prohibition of collective punishment. Violations may constitute war crimes.

Learn more: ICRC — War and Law

THREE ACTIONS.

Crisis Hub exists because people want to help but don't know where to start. Mainstream media coverage is inconsistent — Sudan, Congo, and Myanmar rarely trend. Donation links, political action steps, and educational context are scattered across dozens of sites. This site brings it together.

Not a news site

It's a resource hub that stays useful even when the news cycle moves on.

Not politically aligned

Pro-human-rights, not pro-any-party. Crises of all political origins are documented here.

Not a fundraising platform

We link to vetted organizations but handle no money directly.

OUR PRINCIPLES

01

Cite everything

Every statistic, every claim is linked to a reputable source — UNHCR, WHO, ICRC, Reuters, AP, and more.

02

Never sensationalize

The facts are devastating enough. We state them plainly and let them speak for themselves.

03

Center affected voices

We prioritize journalists, activists, and organizations from the affected regions — not just Western NGOs.

04

Acknowledge complexity

We don't oversimplify conflicts into good/evil narratives. Context is essential for meaningful action.

05

Vet every organization

Before we list a donation link, we research using Charity Navigator, GiveWell, and direct investigation.

06

Stay current

A stale donation link is worse than none. Every page has a 'Last Updated' timestamp.
